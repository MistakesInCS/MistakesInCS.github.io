Hi, this is my blog about stuff that I build or think about. I'm not very good at building or thinking, evidently.

I like computers, I have a degree, I hope AI doesn't prevent me from thinking or building for a living.

My perspective feels like it flips every day. My current hope is that software as an industry (and lots of thinking and building jobs) are less about building the actual thing than taking a noisy, poorly formed thought from someone's head and turning it into a real, physical, and functional thing. 

My current worry is that AI will automate every step of the process that is meaningful in some way, so that all that most humans are left to do is essentially janitorial, and/or that AI will be able to interface with software requesters (human or not) better than humans can.
